# Questions and Answers (Q&A)

**Q: I cannot log in to DigitalOcean, or DigitalOcean will not accept my payment method.**

A: Try logging in (or completing the payment) in a different browser. For example, if you used Chrome, try Firefox or Edge instead. You can also try logging in or completing the payment on your phone. If none of this works, contact DigitalOcean support.
